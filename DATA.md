# Data

No dataset files, images or trained model weights are stored in this repository. They are excluded by `.gitignore` and must be downloaded from their original sources.

## Datasets

| ID | Dataset | Used in the PoC | Licence / terms | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D01 | Conjunctiva image dataset of children from Ghana (anemic / non-anemic eyelid strips, 4,262 files) | Yes: notebooks 1, 2, 4, 5, 6 | CC BY 4.0 | Mendeley Data, https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/nt7r8hv2pz/1 |
| D03 | Eye Conjunctiva Segmentation Dataset (547 photos, masks from 2 annotators) | Yes: notebooks 1, 3 | CC BY 4.0 | Mendeley Data, DOI 10.17632/yxwjgcndg2.1 |
| D02 | EYES-DEFY anemia dataset | **No** | Shared by its author for non-commercial academic research; no redistribution | Not stored or used here |
| D04 | Synthetic damaged copies of D01 (PoC version: val and test only) | Yes: notebooks 4, 5, 6 | Derived from D01 | Generated by notebook 4 (not stored here; reproducible from fixed seeds) |

## Ethics

- Secondary data only; no participants, no new data collection (ethics declaration Class 1).
- D01 images are anonymous pre-cut eyelid strips. D03 photos show faces and are used only to train the eyelid finder; no D03 images are stored in this repository.
- D02 is the locked external test set. It is kept offline on the student's laptop, is not uploaded to Google Drive, Colab or GitHub, and is opened once in the full implementation (experiment E4).
- The software is a research prototype, not a medical device, and is not used to make decisions about real people.

## What is in `poc/results/`

Only derived tables (file names, labels, splits, predictions, quality numbers, decisions) and summary numbers. No images.

| File | From notebook | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| `D01_splits.csv` | 2 | Every D01 file with label, photo group and split |
| `D01_lookalike_checks.csv` | 2 | Look-alike pairs checked, colour difference, joined or not |
| `D01_manual_decisions.csv` | 2 | Borderline pairs reviewed by eye (audit trail) |
| `D03_splits.csv` | 3 | D03 photo split |
| `M2_results.json`, `M2_history.csv` | 3 | Eyelid finder scores and training history |
| `D04_poc_manifest.csv` | 4 | Damaged images: damage, level, seed, simple measures |
| `M4_predictions.csv`, `M4_results.json`, `M4_history.csv` | 5 | Baseline predictions with quality profile, scores, training history |
| `E1_preview_damage_effect.csv` | 5 | AUROC and answers changed per damage and level |
| `ACR_decisions.csv`, `ACR_comparison.csv`, `ACR_thresholds.json` | 6 | Accept / Correct / Reject decisions, comparison of ways of working, limits used |
